Epidemiology · Mental health · Stigma

I want to help build a society where people don’t hold prejudices about each other, where everyone can say, “That’s okay, even if you’re different from me.”

I am a master’s student in epidemiology. I study how childhood family environments shape adolescent mental health, and I am interested in how stigma and social connection affect depression, social anxiety, and autism.
